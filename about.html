Who we are

Fortera Homes is an Ottawa builder and developer. We build multiplexes for landowners and investors, and our own purpose-built rental collections.

Because we hold the buildings we develop, we build them to last and to finance well. That same standard goes into every client project, backed by 25 years of construction experience.

  • Multiplexes for landowners and investors, from approvals to handover.
  • Rental collections we develop, own and lease ourselves.
