Recent provincial and municipal changes allow more homes on many residential lots across Ottawa. A single-family property can often become three, four or more rental units, and a long-term source of income.

We build multi-unit rentals for ourselves, so we know what it takes to get one approved, built and financed, including CMHC MLI Select, and what makes a building easy to rent and hold.

  • Triplexes & fourplexes on existing residential lots.
  • Low-rise rental buildings for larger or combined sites.
  • Additional units: secondary suites and coach houses alongside an existing home.
Foundations
CMHC MLI Select

Built to
finance

We have hands-on experience with CMHC financing for rental buildings, including MLI Select. It’s CMHC’s mortgage loan insurance product for rental buildings with five or more units. Buildings that commit to affordability, energy efficiency or accessibility earn points. More points unlock higher leverage, longer amortization and lower insurance premiums.

The points are won or lost at the design stage. We plan the unit mix, rents, envelope and layouts around a target score from day one, so the finished building qualifies for the financing you planned on. We prepare your financing package and work with your lender or broker to secure it.

The points system

How the
score works

Points come from three outcomes, and you can combine them. A building needs at least 50 points to qualify. The thresholds below are for new construction.

i.

Affordability

A share of units rented at or below 30% of the median renter income for the area, held for at least 10 years.

Level 110% of units50PTS
Level 215% of units70PTS
Level 325% of units100PTS

+30 points for a 20-year commitment

ii.

Energy efficiency

Modelled energy performance beyond the 2020 National Energy Code (NECB) or Building Code (NBC) Tier 1, confirmed by a qualified professional.

Level 125% better than NECB or 20% better than NBC20PTS
Level 250% better than NECB or 40% better than NBC35PTS
Level 360% better than NECB or 70% better than NBC50PTS
iii.

Accessibility

Every unit visitable and common areas barrier-free (CSA B651:23), plus accessible or universal-design units.

Level 115% of units accessible or universal design20PTS
Level 215% accessible + 85% universal design, or 100% universal design30PTS

On a small building

One unit
can be 70 points

The strategy

Build, stabilize,
refinance, hold

Many owners use MLI Select as the take-out loan: construction debt is replaced with a long, low-payment insured mortgage once the building is rented. That frees up equity and keeps cash flow positive for the long hold.

  1. 01Design for a score

    Pick the points target, then set unit count, mix, rents, envelope and layouts to hit it.

  2. 02Finance the build

    A conventional construction loan, CMHC-insured construction financing or CMHC’s Apartment Construction Loan Program.

  3. 03Build and lease up

    We manage the build. Energy and accessibility attestations are completed within 60 days of the last advance.

  4. 04Stabilize

    Rents reach the levels the loan was underwritten on. The appraisal is based on that income.

  5. 05Refinance and hold

    An MLI Select take-out with up to 50-year amortization. Affordability is reported annually through the commitment.

Under five units? MLI Select and CMHC’s construction loan program both start at five. That’s why we check early whether your lot can carry a five- or six-unit building rather than a fourplex. For buildings of four units or fewer, we plan around conventional or small-rental financing instead.

Questions, answered

Multiplex FAQ

Do I have to manage the build myself?

No. We manage the whole project (approvals, permits, trades and inspections) and hand the finished building over to you.

How many units can my lot hold?

It depends on the lot size, zoning and servicing. We start with a zoning and feasibility review that tells you what’s realistically buildable before you spend on design.

Do I need a Committee of Adjustment approval?

Not always. Many multiplexes fit within the zoning as-of-right. If yours needs relief (reduced setbacks or extra height, for example), we prepare the application and support you through the hearing.

Can I live in one unit and rent the others?

Yes. Many owners live in one unit and rent the rest. We plan the layout and unit mix around how you intend to use the building.

Do I need to own the land already?

No. We can review a property you’re considering before you buy, so you know what it can support and roughly what it will cost to build.

What is CMHC MLI Select?

CMHC’s mortgage loan insurance product for rental buildings with five or more units. Commitments to affordability, energy efficiency and accessibility earn points. At 50, 70 or 100 points, a building can qualify for up to 95% loan-to-cost, 40 to 50-year amortization and a 10 to 30% premium discount.

Do you arrange the mortgage?

We prepare your financing package and work with your lender or broker to secure it. Your lender or broker arranges the financing itself.

Does a fourplex qualify for MLI Select?

No. MLI Select requires at least five units (50 for retirement homes). If your lot can support five or six units, we’ll show you how the numbers compare with a fourplex.

What counts as an “affordable” unit?

A unit rented at or below 30% of the median renter household income for the market. The commitment runs at least 10 years from first occupancy, with annual rent increases limited to what provincial legislation allows. A 20-year commitment earns 30 extra points.

How are energy and accessibility points proven?

A qualified professional (an engineer, architect, CET or CEM) attests to the modelled energy savings, and an architect or accessibility consultant attests to the accessibility criteria. For insured construction loans, that documentation is due within 60 days of the last advance. After that there’s no annual reporting for energy or accessibility.

What does CMHC look for in the borrower?

A net worth of at least 25% of the loan amount (minimum $100,000) and five years of experience operating similar rental properties. Without that experience, a contract with a professional property management firm can meet the requirement.

What is the Apartment Construction Loan Program?

ACLP is CMHC’s low-cost construction loan for rental buildings of five or more units, with a minimum loan of $1 million. It funds construction through to stabilized operations, uses its own points scorecard, and can reach up to 100% loan-to-cost and 50-year amortization for the strongest projects.
