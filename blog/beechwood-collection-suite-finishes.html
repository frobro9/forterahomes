Inside the Suites: The Finishes That Set Beechwood Collection Apart

Renters touring older buildings in Ottawa get used to a certain vocabulary — "character," "cozy," "recently updated kitchen" that turns out to mean a new coat of paint. Beechwood Collection was designed around a different premise: that a rental suite should feel as considered as a home you'd choose to buy, not a space you're settling for until something better comes along.

Finishes That Don't Feel Like a Rental

Every suite at Beechwood Collection is finished to the same standard, regardless of floor or layout. That means quartz countertops, quality cabinetry, and modern kitchen appliances throughout — not just in a handful of "premium" units held back for a higher price point. The goal was consistency: what you see in the photos is what you get, in every one of the eight suites.

Bathrooms follow the same logic. Clean tilework, contemporary fixtures, and thoughtful lighting replace the builder-grade finishes that tend to define Ottawa's older rental stock. It's a small thing until you're the one living with it every day.

Light and Ceiling Height Matter More Than People Think

Large windows and higher-than-standard ceilings do more for a space than most renters expect going in. They change how a room reads at every hour of the day, and they make a modestly sized bedroom feel considerably larger than its square footage suggests. At Beechwood Collection, both were treated as design priorities from the earliest planning stages — not value-engineered out once the budget got tight.

The Small Details Renters Notice First

In-suite laundry, private balconies, and dedicated storage are the details that show up in almost every conversation with prospective tenants. They're also the details most commonly missing from Ottawa's older 2 bedroom apartments and 3 bedroom rentals. Beechwood Collection includes all three as standard, because they're the difference between a suite that works for a season and one that works for years.

Built for the Long Term

Fortera Homes approaches finishes the way it approaches construction generally — with an eye toward durability, not just first impressions. Materials were chosen to hold up under real daily use, not just to photograph well for a listing. For renters comparing Beechwood Ottawa rentals against the rest of the market, that difference tends to become obvious the moment they walk through the door.

See the finishes for yourself when Beechwood Collection opens.
