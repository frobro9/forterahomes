Two Bedroom Rentals in Beechwood, Ottawa: What to Expect in 2026

Ottawa's rental landscape is shifting. As demand for quality, spacious housing continues to outpace supply, more renters are looking beyond the usual suspects — Centretown, Hintonburg, the Glebe — and discovering what Beechwood South has to offer. If you're searching for two bedroom rentals in Ottawa, this neighbourhood deserves a serious look.

Why Beechwood, and Why Now?

The Beechwood corridor has long been a favourite among established Ottawa families, but it's increasingly catching the eye of renters who want more than a cramped one-bedroom. The area sits at the intersection of two worlds: the amenity-rich streets of the New Edinburgh and Rockcliffe Park area to the north, and the energy of Vanier to the south. What you get is a genuinely liveable neighbourhood with easy access to both sides of the city.

Beechwood Ottawa rentals are attracting a new wave of tenants: couples upgrading from one-bedrooms, young families looking for their first proper home, and remote workers who need a dedicated office space. The common thread is the need for room — and Beechwood still has it.

What to Expect from Two Bedroom Units

Square Footage and Layout

In 2026, a well-built 2 bedroom apartment in Beechwood Ottawa typically runs between 850 and 950 square feet. At Beechwood Collection, the suites at 82 Genest St. are sized at 860 to 920 sq ft, offering open-concept layouts designed to maximize every square foot.

Finishes and Features

The expectation for new-build rentals in Ottawa has risen sharply. Renters in the two bedroom category now expect in-suite laundry, engineered hardwood or high-end laminate throughout, and kitchens finished with quartz countertops and integrated appliances. High ceilings — typically 9 to 10 feet — are increasingly standard in purpose-built buildings.

Pricing Context

Beechwood Ottawa rentals sit at a premium compared to some inner-city markets, but the value proposition is strong. For a well-finished 2 bedroom apartment in a new building, renters can expect pricing competitive with Westboro and Hintonburg but with meaningfully more square footage per dollar.

What Makes Beechwood Stand Out for Families and Couples

Beyond the units themselves, Beechwood is a neighbourhood that rewards daily life. The main strip along Beechwood Avenue offers an independent coffee shop, a pharmacy, a grocery store, and a stretch of restaurants that punches well above the neighbourhood's size. Stanley Park is a short walk for green space, and the cycling infrastructure along the Ottawa River Parkway connects you to downtown in under 20 minutes on a bike.

For families, the school catchments in the area are consistently well-regarded, and the low-traffic residential streets feel genuinely safe for children. For couples, the dining and evening walk culture makes it feel like a proper urban neighbourhood without the noise and density of the downtown core.

What to Look for When Renting

Not all large units in Ottawa are built equally. When evaluating a 2 bedroom apartment in Beechwood or anywhere in Ottawa, pay close attention to:

  • Storage — in-unit storage, locker, and bike storage matter more in larger units
  • Soundproofing — concrete construction significantly outperforms wood-frame in multi-unit buildings
  • Lease terms — understand what's included (heat, hydro, parking) and what isn't
  • Building management — a responsive landlord or property manager makes a significant difference in daily quality of life

Looking Ahead: New Supply in Beechwood

The Beechwood Collection at 82 Genest St. represents one of the first purpose-built rental developments to come to Beechwood South in years. With only 8 suites — all two bedrooms — and a Spring 2027 completion date, availability will be genuinely limited. For renters who want to secure a large unit in this neighbourhood, early registration is the most practical path forward.
