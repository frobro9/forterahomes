Take-Out Financing: Replacing Your Construction Loan After Lease-Up

A construction loan is built to be replaced. The take-out is the long-term mortgage that pays it off. For most rental buildings, it's where the real value of the project shows up.

What is take-out financing?

Take-out financing (also called permanent or exit financing) is the long-term mortgage that replaces a construction loan once a building is complete and rented. Construction loans are short, carry higher rates and are often interest-only. The take-out is a term mortgage with a long amortization, sized on the building's actual income.

The sequence

  1. Completion and occupancy: the building is finished and an occupancy permit is issued.
  2. Lease-up: units are rented. New buildings often take a few months to fill, especially in a softer market.
  3. Stabilization: rents reach the levels the loan was underwritten on, with normal vacancy.
  4. Appraisal and underwriting: the lender values the building on stabilized net operating income and sizes the loan on value and debt coverage.
  5. Funding: the take-out pays off the construction loan. Any surplus can return equity to the owner.

MLI Select as the take-out

For buildings of five or more units, CMHC MLI Select is often the strongest take-out available. It offers up to 95% of value, amortization of 40, 45 or 50 years depending on points, and premium discounts of 10% to 30%. Premiums for a take-out ("all other loan purposes") are lower than for construction financing. For standard rental, they range from 2.60% at 65% loan-to-value to 6.15% above 90%, plus 0.25% for every five years of amortization beyond 25.

A rough example at more than 90% loan-to-value with a 50-year amortization: 6.15% + 1.25% = 7.40%, less the 30% discount at 100 points ≈ 5.18% of the loan. At 70 points and 45 years, it's ≈ 5.72%. The premium can be added to the loan.

Guarantees and holdbacks

For construction or completion take-out financing under MLI Select, the borrower and guarantor guarantee 100% of the loan until projected rents have been achieved and stabilized for 12 consecutive months. After that, the guarantee may drop to 40%. At 100 points, CMHC may consider limited recourse. On new construction, a rental achievement holdback may apply: part of the loan is held back until target rents are reached.

Refinancing later

If you refinance an existing CMHC-insured loan, CMHC may credit part of the original premium: 75% within one year, scaling down to 20% at seven years. It's worth planning the first term with the next refinance in mind.

Plan the take-out before you build

The take-out is decided by choices made years earlier: the unit count, the mix, the rents you commit to, the energy model and the accessibility layout. If those don't line up with the program you're counting on, the building can come in under its expected value. We plan the take-out at the feasibility stage and design to it. We prepare your financing package and work with your lender or broker to secure it.

Questions

When can I get take-out financing on a new rental building?

Typically once the building is complete, leased up and its income has stabilized. Lenders size the loan on the stabilized income.

Can I take out equity when I refinance a new build?

If the stabilized value supports a loan larger than the construction loan, the take-out can return equity, subject to loan-to-value and debt coverage limits.

Are CMHC premiums lower for a take-out than for construction financing?

Yes. CMHC's premium schedule for standard rental housing is lower for 'all other loan purposes' than for construction financing at every loan-to-value level.

Sources: CMHC MLI Select. Program terms are set by CMHC and can change; confirm current terms with your lender.
