Finding Affordable Large Units in Ottawa: A Renter's Guide to Beechwood

Ottawa's rental market has tightened considerably over the past few years. Vacancy rates have dropped, prices have risen, and the supply of genuinely large rental units — the kind with room for a proper dining table, a home office, and a guest bedroom — has failed to keep pace with demand. But large units do still exist if you know where to look. And for renters who prioritize space, Beechwood is increasingly the answer.

Why Large Units Are So Hard to Find in Ottawa

Most of Ottawa's new rental construction has targeted the studio and one-bedroom market, where per-square-foot returns are highest and unit turnover is quickest. The result is an acute shortage of affordable large units in Ottawa — particularly 2 bedroom apartments in Ottawa that are actually designed for families rather than converted from smaller floor plans.

The few large units that do come to market are typically found in older stock (often with outdated finishes), high-rises with amenity fees that push overall costs up, or in neighbourhoods far enough from the core that commute times become a real concern.

Beechwood as a Solution for Space-Seeking Renters

Newer Buildings, Better Layouts

What makes Beechwood particularly interesting for renters seeking large rental units in Beechwood is the neighbourhood's recent new construction. Unlike older buildings where 2 bedrooms often means 700 square feet with awkward room shapes, the new purpose-built rentals arriving in Beechwood South are designed specifically around how families and couples actually live.

At the Beechwood Collection, for example, the two bedroom suites at 82 Genest St. run from 860 to 920 square feet with open-concept kitchen and living areas, full-sized bedrooms, and proper storage — a layout that works for a couple who works from home, or a small family who needs space to breathe.

Value Relative to Comparable Areas

Beechwood's relative obscurity in the Ottawa rental conversation has kept prices from reaching the levels seen in the Glebe or Westboro. For affordable large units in Ottawa, the Beechwood corridor offers meaningfully more space per rental dollar than many comparably located neighbourhoods, while still delivering the walkability, transit access, and community infrastructure that renters rightly demand.

What to Look For When Searching for Large Units

Define "Large" for Your Life

Before your search begins, be precise about what you need. A 2 bedroom apartment in Ottawa at 850 sq ft works very differently than one at 700 sq ft. Ask for the actual square footage (including balconies and storage — which some listings include to inflate the number) and the layout diagram. An L-shaped open plan with separate bedrooms on opposite sides of the unit is very different from two bedrooms sharing a wall with a galley kitchen.

Prioritize Purpose-Built Over Converted

Many "2 bedroom" units in older Ottawa buildings are conversions — originally one-bedrooms or bachelor units that had walls added to create additional rooms. These rarely offer the light, ventilation, or usable square footage of a unit designed from scratch for two bedrooms. Purpose-built rentals, like new construction in Beechwood South, are the safer bet for genuine space.

Register Early for New Developments

The most accessible path to a quality large unit in Ottawa — particularly in desirable neighbourhoods — is early registration. New rental buildings announce their units before construction completes, and early registrants are typically given first access to the best available suites. Given the limited supply of two bedroom apartments in Ottawa in well-built new buildings, this matters more than most renters realize.

The Beechwood Opportunity

With only 8 suites in the Beechwood Collection, availability will be genuinely constrained. For renters who have been searching for a large, well-finished unit in a walkable Ottawa neighbourhood without the premium pricing of the most in-demand areas, 82 Genest St. represents a real window.
