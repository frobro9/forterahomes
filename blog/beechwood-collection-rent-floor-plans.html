Beechwood Collection Rent & Floor Plans: 2 Bedroom Apartments in Beechwood, Ottawa

Rent at Beechwood Collection starts at $2,300 per month. The building at 82 Genest St. in Beechwood South, Ottawa has eight suites, and every one is a two bedroom home between 860 and 920 sq ft. It is now leasing, with move-ins starting early 2027.

Rent and Floor Plans at a Glance

  • Mackay: 2 bed / 1 bath, 860 sq ft, ground floor (units 101 and 102): $2,300/month
  • Manor: 2 bed / 2 bath, 920 sq ft, 2nd and 3rd floor (units 201, 202, 301, 302): $2,500/month
  • Rideau: 2 bed / 2 bath, 920 sq ft, top floor (units 401 and 402): $2,600/month

You can see the full floor plan for every unit on the Beechwood Collection page.

Mackay: Ground Floor, 2 Bed / 1 Bath

The two Mackay suites sit at ground level with easy at-grade access. Each has an open kitchen, living and dining area, a primary bedroom with a large closet, a secondary bedroom, a full bathroom, and in-suite laundry. Each has a private terrace. At $2,300/month, Mackay is the lowest rent in the building.

Manor: 2nd and 3rd Floor, 2 Bed / 2 Bath

The four Manor suites add a second full bathroom: a main bath plus a private ensuite off the primary bedroom. The open kitchen, living and dining area is a little larger, the primary bedroom is about 122 sq ft, and each suite has its own balcony. Manor rent is $2,500/month.

Rideau: Top Floor, 2 Bed / 2 Bath

The two Rideau suites share the Manor layout, with two full bathrooms and a balcony, on the building’s top floor. Rideau rent is $2,600/month.

What’s Included in Every Suite

  • In-suite washer and dryer
  • High ceilings, large windows, and premium kitchen finishes
  • A private balcony or patio
  • Bell Fiber internet (over 1 Gbps) included for leases signed on or before December 31, 2026, a $110/month value
  • Keyless entry, HRV ventilation, and a smart thermostat
  • Dedicated storage units and bicycle storage in the building

Lease Terms and How to Apply

Beechwood Collection offers 1 year, 2 year, and 3 year or longer leases. To apply, fill out the short form on the application page. The Fortera Homes team follows up with availability, and approved applicants choose their floor and unit. Screening includes a credit check plus a look at rental history, income, and references.

Where It Is

82 Genest St. is in Beechwood South, at the edge of New Edinburgh, a short walk from the cafés and shops on Beechwood Avenue and about two minutes from the Ottawa River Pathway. The area has a Walk Score of 94 and a Bike Score of 99, and OC Transpo buses run into downtown Ottawa.

Beechwood Collection is now leasing for early 2027.
