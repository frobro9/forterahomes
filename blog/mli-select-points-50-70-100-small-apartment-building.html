How to Score 50, 70 or 100 MLI Select Points on a Small Apartment Building

On a small building, a single unit can move a project from 50 to 70 points. Here's how the math works, and how to choose the combination that costs the least.

MLI Select awards points for affordability, energy efficiency and accessibility. You need 50 to qualify, and the benefits step up at 70 and 100. (For the full rules, see MLI Select explained.) The thresholds are percentages of total units, which works in favour of small buildings.

Affordable units needed, by building size

New construction thresholds are 10% of units (50 points), 15% (70) and 25% (100). Rounded up to whole units:

On five- and six-unit buildings, one affordable unit reaches 70 points. On an eight-unit building, two units reach 100. CMHC also reviews which unit types you designate, so designating only the smallest unit won't necessarily be accepted.

Choosing the cheapest route to your target

Affordability costs rent

Each affordable unit earns less than market rent for at least 10 years, which lowers net operating income. Since loans are sized on income, the trade only pays if the longer amortization and lower premium are worth more than the rent you give up. On small buildings, where one unit is a big share, it often is.

Energy costs construction dollars, once

Level 1 and 2 energy targets are often reachable with a good envelope, heat pumps and heat recovery ventilation. Level 3 (70% better than the NBC for Part 9 buildings) is a much higher bar and needs energy modelling early. Unlike affordability, the commitment ends once it's attested.

Accessibility is mostly a layout decision

Visitability and barrier-free common areas apply at every level, so ground-floor access, door widths and washroom layouts need to be planned from the first sketch. Universal-design units can earn 20 or 30 points without giving up rent.

An example: six units in Ottawa

A six-unit building with one affordable unit for 10 years reaches 70 points: up to 45-year amortization and a 20% premium discount. Add Accessibility Level 2 (all units universal design) and it reaches 100 points: 50-year amortization, a 30% discount and limited recourse. That's with one affordable unit out of six.

How we plan it

We run these trade-offs at the feasibility stage: the rent given up, the construction cost of each energy level, and the loan each scenario supports. Then we design to the winning score, so the attestations line up at completion. We prepare your financing package and work with your lender or broker to secure it.

Questions

How many affordable units does a six-unit building need for MLI Select?

One affordable unit (about 17% of units) meets the 15% threshold for 70 points on new construction. Two units (33%) meet the 25% threshold for 100 points.

Can I reach 100 MLI Select points without affordable units?

No. Energy efficiency (up to 50) and accessibility (up to 30) total at most 80 points. Reaching 100 requires an affordability commitment.

What is the 20-year affordability bonus?

Committing to affordability for 20 years or more instead of 10 earns an additional 30 points.
