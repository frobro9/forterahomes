Debt Coverage Ratio (DCR) Explained for Ottawa Rental Buildings

Rental buildings borrow against their income, not your salary. The debt coverage ratio is the number that turns rents into a loan amount.

What is the debt coverage ratio?

The debt coverage ratio (DCR), also called the debt service coverage ratio (DSCR), compares a building's income to its mortgage payments:

DCR = net operating income ÷ annual debt service

Net operating income (NOI) is rent and other income, less a vacancy allowance and operating costs (property tax, insurance, utilities, repairs, management). Annual debt service is twelve months of mortgage payments. A DCR of 1.10 means the building earns $1.10 for every $1.00 of mortgage payments.

CMHC's minimums

For standard rental housing, CMHC's MLI Select and the Apartment Construction Loan Program both require a minimum DCR of 1.10. Other shelter models need 1.20, and non-residential space 1.40. Lenders and CMHC may test the ratio at a qualifying rate higher than your actual rate. The ACLP qualifies at the higher of 2.00% or CMHC's indicative rate plus 1.00%.

A worked example

Take a building with a stabilized NOI of $150,000. At a 1.10 DCR, the most it can pay in mortgage payments is $150,000 ÷ 1.10 = $136,364 a year, or about $11,364 a month. How much that payment can borrow depends on the amortization:

25 years≈ $2.16M≈ $2.05M
40 years (MLI Select, 50 pts)≈ $2.73M≈ $2.54M
45 years (MLI Select, 70 pts)≈ $2.86M≈ $2.65M
50 years (MLI Select, 100 pts)≈ $2.96M≈ $2.73M

Illustrative only: semi-annual compounding, no qualifying-rate stress test, before insurance premiums. The loan is also capped by loan-to-value or loan-to-cost limits.

On the same rents, stretching from 25 to 50 years supports roughly $700,000 to $800,000 more borrowing. That's why MLI Select's amortization tiers matter as much as its leverage.

What moves the number

  • Rents and unit mix: family-sized two- and three-bedroom units are scarce in Ottawa and hold their value.
  • Operating costs: efficient envelopes and heat pumps lower utility costs, and newer systems mean fewer repairs.
  • Amortization: the longer it is, the lower the payment.
  • Rate: insured loans typically carry lower rates than uninsured ones.

There's a trade-off with affordability: MLI Select affordable units earn points but bring in less rent. The goal is the combination that maximizes the loan, not just the rent roll. We model both before design is locked.

The takeaway

When you plan a rental building, design for the DCR, not just the rent. Lenders will. We prepare your financing package and work with your lender or broker to secure it.

Questions

What DCR does CMHC require?

A minimum of 1.10 for standard rental housing under MLI Select and the ACLP, 1.20 for other shelter models and 1.40 for non-residential space.

Is DCR the same as DSCR?

Yes. Debt coverage ratio and debt service coverage ratio describe the same calculation: net operating income divided by annual debt payments.

Does a longer amortization increase how much I can borrow?

Yes. A longer amortization lowers the annual payment, so the same income supports a larger loan at the same DCR, subject to loan-to-value limits.

Sources: CMHC MLI Select · CMHC ACLP Program Highlight Sheet. Program terms are set by CMHC and can change; confirm current terms with your lender.
