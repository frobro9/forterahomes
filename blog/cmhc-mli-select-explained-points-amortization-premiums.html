CMHC MLI Select Explained: Points, 50-Year Amortization and Premium Discounts

MLI Select has become the most important financing tool for new rental buildings in Canada. It rewards three things (affordability, energy efficiency and accessibility) with better loan terms.

What is MLI Select?

CMHC MLI Select is a multi-unit mortgage loan insurance product. It took effect in March 2022, replacing MLI Flex. CMHC insures the loan, which lets approved lenders offer higher leverage, longer amortization and lower rates than they would on an uninsured loan. MLI Select scales those flexibilities to the social outcomes a project commits to, measured in points.

Eligibility

  • New and existing standard rental housing, single-room occupancy, supportive housing and retirement homes. Student housing qualifies only through energy efficiency and accessibility.
  • A minimum of five units (50 units or beds for retirement homes).
  • Non-residential space no more than 30% of gross floor area and 30% of lending value.
  • At least 50 points.

The points: new construction

Affordability

Units with rents at or below 30% of the median renter income for the market, maintained for at least 10 years from first occupancy:

LevelShare of unitsPoints
110%50
215%70
325%100

A commitment of 20 years or more earns an additional 30 points. For existing buildings, the thresholds are 40%, 60% and 80% of units.

Energy efficiency

LevelTargetPoints
125% better than NECB Tier 1, or 20% better than NBC Tier 120
250% better than NECB, or 40% better than NBC35
360% better than NECB, or 70% better than NBC50

Performance is measured against the 2020 national codes and attested by a qualified professional (P.Eng., architect, CET or CEM) using approved energy modelling software.

Accessibility

All levels require every unit to be visitable and common areas barrier-free under CSA B651:23.

  • Level 1 (20 points): at least 15% of units accessible or universal design, or Rick Hansen Foundation Accessibility Certification (60–79%).
  • Level 2 (30 points): 15% accessible plus 85% universal design, 100% universal design or 100% accessible, or RHFAC Gold (80%+).

What the points unlock

50+95%40 years10%Full

For existing properties, 50 points allows up to 85% loan-to-value, and 70+ up to 95%. Replacement reserves are discretionary. The minimum debt coverage ratio for standard rental is 1.10.

Premiums and fees

CMHC's premium depends on loan-to-value and loan purpose. Above 90% loan-to-value (available only under MLI Select), the base premium for standard rental is 7.00% for construction financing and 6.15% for other purposes such as a take-out. Each five years of amortization beyond 25 adds 0.25%. The points discount applies to the base premium plus surcharges. Application fees for five or more units are $150 per unit (up to two advances) or $200 per unit (more than two advances) for the first 100 units.

Borrower requirements

  • Net worth of at least 25% of the loan (minimum $100,000). CMHC may be flexible at 100+ points.
  • At least five years' experience managing similar rental property, or a formal contract with a professional property manager.
  • For construction and completion take-outs, a 100% guarantee until rents are stabilized for 12 consecutive months, which may then reduce to 40%.

Staying compliant

Affordability is ongoing: rents must stay at or below the threshold for the full commitment, increases are limited to what provincial legislation allows, and compliance is documented annually. Energy and accessibility are one-time. Once the attestation is filed (within 60 days of the last advance on insured construction financing), there are no further requirements.

How we use it

We have hands-on experience with MLI Select, and we plan buildings around a target score from the first feasibility study. The right mix of affordability, envelope performance and layout decides how much a building can borrow. Our guide to scoring 50, 70 or 100 points shows the common combinations. Questions

What is the minimum number of units for MLI Select?

Five units for standard rental housing, or 50 units or beds for retirement homes.

How many points do I need for a 50-year amortization?

At least 100 points. 50 points allows up to 40 years and 70 points up to 45 years.

What is the MLI Select premium discount?

10% at 50 points, 20% at 70 points and 30% at 100 points, applied to the base premium plus any surcharges.

How is 'affordable' defined under MLI Select?

Rent at or below 30% of the median renter household income for the market, maintained for at least 10 years.
