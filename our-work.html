Our Work

What we build
& how we build it

From planning and approvals to concrete and framing: a look at the work our team delivers on site.

Capabilities

Every stage,
in house

From the first look at the land to handing over the keys, we plan, manage and build every stage with our own team and trusted trades.

01Planning & approvals

Site selection, feasibility, zoning review, Committee of Adjustment applications and permits.

02Design

Layouts, elevations and finishes planned with our design partners for how people actually live.

03Site work

Demolition, excavation, utilities and grading, done safely on tight urban sites.

04Concrete & foundations

Footings, foundation walls and slabs, poured and inspected to spec.

05Structure

Floor systems, wall panels and roof framing, set with crane support to keep schedules tight.

06Envelope & exterior

Roofing, windows, insulation and cladding, sealed tight and built for Ottawa winters.

07Systems & interiors

Heat pumps, HRV ventilation, plumbing and electrical, then kitchens, baths, flooring and millwork.

08Completion & handover

Final inspections, a full walkthrough and a finished building, ready to live in or lease.
