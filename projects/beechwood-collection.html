Living in Beechwood, Ottawa.

Positioned in one of Ottawa's most walkable and dynamic communities, 82 Genest St. offers unmatched connectivity and urban convenience.

  • 3 minute walk to JnJ Bakery Café and neighbourhood coffee spots.
  • 2 minute walk to the Ottawa River Pathway for running and cycling.
  • Steps to OC Transpo bus service into downtown Ottawa.
  • Minutes from Beechwood Cemetery’s 160 acres of walking trails.
