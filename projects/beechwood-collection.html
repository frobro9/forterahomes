Questions, answered

Beechwood FAQ

How much is rent at Beechwood Collection?

Rent starts at $2,300 per month. Mackay suites (2 bed / 1 bath, 860 sq ft, ground floor) are $2,300/month, Manor suites (2 bed / 2 bath, 920 sq ft, 2nd and 3rd floor) are $2,500/month, and Rideau suites (2 bed / 2 bath, 920 sq ft, top floor) are $2,600/month.

When can I move in?

Beechwood Collection is now leasing, with move-ins starting early 2027. Applying now lets you lock in your rent and choose your floor and unit before the building opens.

Are all the suites two bedrooms?

Yes. All 8 suites are two bedroom homes from 860 to 920 sq ft. Ground floor suites have one full bathroom; suites on the 2nd to 4th floors have two full bathrooms.

Is internet included?

Yes. Bell Fiber internet with speeds over 1 Gbps is included at no extra cost for leases signed on or before December 31, 2026, a $110/month value.

What lease terms are available?

You can choose a 1 year, 2 year, or 3 year or longer lease.

What is included in each suite?

Every suite includes an in-suite washer and dryer, high ceilings, premium kitchen finishes, a private balcony or patio, keyless entry, HRV ventilation, and a smart thermostat. The building also has dedicated storage units and bicycle storage.

Where is Beechwood Collection?

Beechwood Collection is at 82 Genest St. in Beechwood South, Ottawa, a short walk from the cafés and shops on Beechwood Avenue and about two minutes from the Ottawa River Pathway. OC Transpo buses run into downtown Ottawa.

How do I apply?

Fill out the short application form on this page. Our team follows up with availability and next steps. Screening includes a quick credit check plus a look at your rental history, income, and references.
